Shimbashi Night

On request
Kimono bar-hopping

An evening of bar-hopping in Shimbashi, the after-work heart of Tokyo, with a guide in kimono. We meet at SL Square and wander into the izakaya lanes by the Karasumori exit. Not a tourist strip, just Tokyo's real after-work night.

What you'll do

We start with a toast at a sit-down izakaya and try the day's specials. Yuri takes care of the little alleys, how to walk in without breaking the regulars' easy mood, and how to order. You can add one slightly special stop, like fugu or seasonal small plates. We end by the steam locomotive at SL Square, talking over the night.

What's included

Your private guide, help choosing and booking places, and help with ordering. Food and drinks are paid on the day, at cost.

Good to know

This is not a stand-all-night tour. Non-drinkers enjoy it just as much with soft drinks. Solo travelers are welcome.

  • Sit-down bars
  • Guided in kimono
  • Small groups, solo welcome

Kiyosumi Art Walk

On request
Museum, garden and a classic lunch

The daytime tour Yuri, an art lover, recommends most. Take your time at the Museum of Contemporary Art Tokyo and Kiyosumi Gardens in Kiyosumi-Shirakawa, then lunch at a long-loved local spot. Quiet, green, and light on walking.

What you'll do

At the museum, Yuri picks the highlights from the special and collection exhibitions. Then a loop of Kiyosumi Gardens, slowly. We rest by the pond and talk about the stones and pines. Lunch is a teppan set meal at Fukagawatei, a long-standing local favorite.

What's included

Your private guide, route planning, and guiding on the day. Admission fees and meals are extra, paid at cost.

Good to know

A knee-friendly pace with breaks along the way. Not held on Mondays, when the museum is closed.

  • Museum and garden
  • Plenty of rest stops
  • Lunch at a local classic

Kagurazaka to Shimbashi

On request
An afternoon stroll, then an evening hop

Walk the stone-paved lanes of Kagurazaka in the late afternoon, then ride over to Shimbashi for a drink. Tokyo by day and Tokyo by night, in one outing.

What you'll do

We stop at Akagi Shrine, then wander down Kagurazaka, peeking into the stone-paved alleys. Early dinner is ramen at Menya Fuufuutei, a popular Kagurazaka spot. Then one train to Shimbashi: SL Square lit up at night, and a last drink at an izakaya. One train ride and short walks, by design.

What's included

Your private guide, and help choosing places and ordering. Food, drinks and train fares are extra, paid at cost.

Good to know

We take Kagurazaka's slopes at an easy pace. On rainy days we can switch to a shorter, Shimbashi-only version.

  • Alleys and lanes
  • Just one train ride
  • Daylight into evening

A note from Yuri

Hi, I'm Yuri

I live in Kanazawa and work as a nurse.

In Tokyo, I love the stretch from Shinjuku to Shimbashi. In the bar streets of Shimbashi you can almost hear people saying “otsukaresama” (good work today) on their way home, and I never get tired of it. Walking in kimono changes how the city looks, just a little. I'd love to share that feeling with you.

I love art, too. Walking around neighborhoods with museums is how I spend my days off. In Shirakawa-go, people I guided asked me to guide them again.

My knees aren't the best, so I build my tours around two things: places to sit, and no rushing. If walking worries you, come just as you are.

When a booking comes in, I travel from Kanazawa to Tokyo. I'm looking forward to meeting you.
